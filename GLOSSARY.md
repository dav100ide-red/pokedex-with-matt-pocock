# Pokédex

The Pokédex is this app: a list of Pokémon that the user can browse, add, edit and delete.

## Language

### Pokémon

**Pokédex**:
The app, and the collection of Pokémon it manages.
_Avoid_: Pokémon list, roster

**Pokémon**:
One entry in the Pokédex. No two Pokémon share a name; upper and lower case don't count as a difference. The word is the same in the singular and the plural.
_Avoid_: Pokemons, entry, record, row

**Type**:
An elemental category, such as Fire or Water. Every Pokémon has one or two.
_Avoid_: Kind, element, category

**Move**:
A technique a Pokémon knows, such as Tackle. Every Pokémon knows one to four different moves.
_Avoid_: Attack, skill, ability

**Level**:
A whole number from 1 to 100 that says how strong a Pokémon is.
_Avoid_: LV, rank

**Capture date**:
The calendar day a Pokémon was caught. It has no time of day and is never later than today.
_Avoid_: Capture time, catch date, captured at

**Favorite**:
A mark the user puts on a Pokémon they care about most.
_Avoid_: Starred, pinned, bookmarked

### Images

**Image**:
The picture of a Pokémon. A Pokémon may have none.
_Avoid_: Sprite, avatar, photo

**Placeholder image**:
The Poké Ball picture shown in place of a Pokémon's Image when it has none or its Image can't be loaded.
_Avoid_: Default image, fallback image

### Filtering

**Type filter**:
A set of chosen Types that narrows the Pokédex to the Pokémon matching it, by Match any or Match all. With no Types chosen, every Pokémon is shown.
_Avoid_: Type search

**Match any**:
A Type filter mode in which a Pokémon matches if it has at least one of the chosen Types.
_Avoid_: OR

**Match all**:
A Type filter mode in which a Pokémon matches if it has every chosen Type; it may have other Types too.
_Avoid_: AND, exact match
